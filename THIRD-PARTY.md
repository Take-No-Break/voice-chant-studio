# Third-party materials

World of Warcraft names and spell artwork are third-party game materials, not authored by this project. `assets/icons/index.json` records icon source URLs and hashes; spell metadata uses Wowhead Forever. Descriptive Japanese labels and chants are not official localization. No Blizzard affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

Packaged Python dependencies have notices under third-party-licenses/. No project-wide redistribution license has been selected for this initial publication; do not interpret third-party notices as granting rights to unrelated materials.
